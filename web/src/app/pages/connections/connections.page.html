When Stonks trades

A broker that only reads

Stonks reads positions, cash and activity. It never places an order there, whatever your follows or your portfolio's stage.

A broker that can trade, such as Interactive Brokers

Stonks places orders only in the portfolio linked to this account, and only for:

  • strategies you follow there with Approve each trade, after you approve each ticket;
  • strategies you follow there with Automatic;
  • orders you place by hand there, or suggested orders you approve.
