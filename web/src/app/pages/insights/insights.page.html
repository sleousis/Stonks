What these figures mean
Beta
How much your portfolio tends to move with the benchmark. 1 moves in step, 0.5 moves half as much, 0 ignores it. "Covered" is the share of holdings with enough history to measure it.
Gross exposure
Long plus short positions as a share of value: how much is invested. 10,000 with 6,000 long and 2,000 short is 80%.
Net exposure
Long minus short: which way it leans. The same example is 40%.
Largest holding
The weight of your biggest position, and which one it is.
